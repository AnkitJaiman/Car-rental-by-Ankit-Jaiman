Questions, answered

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not at this time. We ship exclusively to addresses in the 50 US states and Washington, D.C. We do not ship to P.O. boxes or international addresses.

Orders placed before 2 PM ET ship the same business day via insured FedEx 2-Day. A signature is required on delivery.

What is your return policy?

Return any unworn, unengraved pendant within 30 days for a full refund. We email you a free prepaid US return label.

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds have the same crystal structure, hardness and brilliance as mined diamonds. They are graded by the same independent labs.
