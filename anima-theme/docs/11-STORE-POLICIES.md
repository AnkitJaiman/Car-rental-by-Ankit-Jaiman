## 1. Refund policy

**Returns**
We want you to love your jewelry. If you are not completely happy, you can return unworn items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the item price.
- Items must be unworn, undamaged and returned in their original packaging.
- Returns are free: we send you a prepaid return label.
- To start a return, email support@animajewel.store with your order number. Please contact us before sending anything back; parcels sent without a return request cannot be accepted.

**Exchanges**
Need a different size or metal? Email us before returning your item and we will help you arrange an exchange.

**Items we cannot accept**
- Worn, damaged or altered items, or items without their original packaging.
- Personalized or custom-made pieces, unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- Gift cards.

**Damaged or wrong items**
If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will replace or refund it at no cost to you.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days; your bank may take a few extra days to show it. Original shipping charges, if any, are not refundable.

**Order cancellations**
You can cancel an order within 24 hours of placing it, as long as it has not yet shipped. Use our Cancel an Order page (animajewel.store/pages/cancel-order) or email us with your order number.

**Questions**
Email support@animajewel.store. We reply within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday.

## 2. Shipping policy

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to addresses within the United States only.

**Shipping costs**
- Free standard shipping on orders over $35.
- For orders under $35, a flat shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

**Processing and delivery times**
- Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding holidays).
- Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by carrier delays, customs or weather.

**Tracking**
You will receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

**Duties and taxes**
Any applicable sales tax is calculated and shown at checkout. If any import duties are due on your order, we will tell you before you complete your purchase.

**Address and delivery problems**
Please check your shipping address carefully. If you notice a mistake, email us as soon as possible and we will try to update it before dispatch. If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, contact us and we will help resolve it.

**Questions**
Email support@animajewel.store.
