# Agent 1: Luxury UI/UX design audit (round 1)

Theme: Anima Editorial (`/home/user/Car-rental-by-Ankit-Jaiman/anima-theme`)
Method: I reviewed every shot in `shots-r1/` (1440px and 390px). I re-measured the live preview at http://127.0.0.1:8787 with Playwright (bounding boxes, computed styles, hover states) and traced each defect back to its source. Placeholder images, the portrait logo placeholder and payment-icon placeholders are known sandbox limits and are not reported.

## Verdict

**Not yet at Mejuri, Catbird or Monica Vinader level. The bones are good: restrained palette, Playfair and Assistant pairing, sharp 0px corners, generous section padding and a calm PDP. But two structural CSS/markup bugs break the polish on every page.**

1. The header section's schema class collides with the `.section-header` utility. As a result, the header never spans the viewport (1245px of 1440, and 358px of 390 on mobile). A white band of about 40px appears under it, and the mobile logo sits 16px off-centre.
2. `product-card.liquid` never closes `.product-card__media-wrap`. On desktop the hover "Choose options" bar lands on top of the title and price of every card. On mobile a bordered full-width button sits between the image and the title of every card, which reads as a marketplace.

The Inspiration Station is the strongest section and close to the brief. Still, it currently reads as "symmetric pyramid plus Instagram overlay" rather than an asymmetric fashion editorial. Its size pattern also breaks at the repeat seam.

## Inspiration Station: detailed notes

- **What works:** a white background, 20px gap, 14px rounded image corners against 0px corners elsewhere (a deliberate contrast), centre-snap scrolling, a mobile start centred on the lead card, a thin progress bar plus arrows, and live product title and price under each image. The centre card is prominent at 1440px.
- **What breaks the brief:**
  - Symmetric pyramid instead of an asymmetric stagger (A1-04).
  - Seam duplication (A1-04).
  - Gradient and creator overlay make it look like an Instagram widget (A1-05).
  - Bordered rounded product boxes (A1-06).
  - At viewports other than about 1440 the "central" lg card is not centred, because the desktop track starts left-aligned (`scrollLeft 0`). Consider applying the `start_centered` behaviour on desktop too, or always placing the lg card first after a short sm/md lead-in.
- **Mobile:** scale 0.72 makes sm cards about 158px wide, so a 52px thumb plus title leaves about 80px of text. On mobile use only md/lg sizes (scale sm up to ≥0.85) or the existing `mobile_layout: uniform`.

## Top 5 must-fix

1. **A1-01**: rename the header schema class (`header.liquid:219`). It currently breaks header width, logo centring and adds a 40px gap on every page.
2. **A1-02 / A1-03**: close `.product-card__media-wrap` in `product-card.liquid` (after line 78) and drop the text quick-add on mobile. Title and price are covered on hover, and the mobile grid looks like a marketplace.
3. **A1-04 / A1-05**: rework the Inspiration Station rhythm to an asymmetric, seam-safe pattern (and fix the dead `"wave"` value). Remove the gradient and creator overlay so it reads as an editorial, not an Instagram feed.
4. **A1-11 / A1-10**: stop linking empty collections from a 10-item nav, and give empty collections a real empty state instead of "No products match these filters".
5. **A1-08 / A1-09**: fix the spotlight image overflowing its band, and stop the two homepage product rows from showing the same four products with mismatched gutters.
