# Agent 4: E-commerce and Conversion Audit, Round 1

**Theme:** Anima Editorial (Shopify OS 2.0), Anima Jewels
**Method:** I used Playwright against http://127.0.0.1:8787 at 1440x900 (desktop) and 390x844 (mobile, touch emulation). I clicked through every flow listed below and checked the theme source for root causes. Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Car-rental-by-Ankit-Jaiman/fdadd1fb-8cbc-5ec2-9d60-60529d96a2a6/scratchpad/shots-r1/` (files named `a4-*`).

**What I tested**
- **Header nav, desktop:** hovered and clicked "Rings".
- **Mobile menu:** opened it and expanded Rings.
- **Homepage:** section order on `/` and `/index-demo.html`.
- **Desktop quick add:** "Choose options" → modal → Add to bag → cart drawer.
- **Mobile quick add:** real tap, then a JS-forced click.
- **PDP:** cycled through all 10 Color × Ring size combinations, checked price, URL, button and stock text. Also: quantity +, Add to bag → drawer, size guide link, and the accordions.
- **Mobile PDP:** fold positions and sticky elements while scrolling.
- **Cart:** drawer contents and tap sizes, `/cart`, `/cart-empty.html`.
- **Collections:** filters drawer and sort on `/collections/rings`, plus the empty `/collections/necklaces`.
- **Search modal:** opened it and typed a query.
- **Inspiration Station (demo):** card → product path.

Placeholder images, missing reviews and the mocked cart contents are out of scope.

## Verdict

**Purchasable, but leaky. Fix the mobile issues before launch.**

The core purchase path works on desktop:
- Quick add opens the modal, Add to bag works, and the drawer opens.
- Selecting a variant updates `?variant=` and the variant id.
- The drawer has a free-shipping message and a clear Checkout button.

PDP copy is strong: trust bullets sit right under the CTA, and the materials facts (carat weight, metal weight) are good for jewelry.

Mobile is where the problems are, and that is where most traffic for this price point will come from:
- The quick-add button on product cards **doesn't work on touch**. Taps go to the PDP instead.
- On the PDP, price and Add to bag sit below the first screen, and no sticky buy bar replaces them.
- The quick-add modal hides its CTA below the fold.

Navigation also points shoppers at empty collections and duplicates "Catalog". Jewelry basics are missing too: gift messaging, an in-context size guide, payment and installment messaging near the CTA, and a delivery estimate.

No Critical issues: every product can still be bought through the PDP.

## Top 5 must-fix

1. **C4-01:** Fix the mobile quick-add hit area (`base.css` ~line 362: `position: relative; z-index: 3`). Today every mobile "Choose options" tap goes to the PDP instead.
2. **C4-02:** Add a sticky mobile Add-to-bag bar and pull the title and price above the fold on the mobile PDP.
3. **C4-04:** Remove or replace the dead Necklaces (wrong handle `necklace`) and Earrings links, and give empty collections a real "coming soon + shop rings" state instead of "No products match these filters".
4. **C4-05 + C4-11:** Restructure the nav around the real catalog: Engagement Rings and Montana Sapphire top-level, Gifts as a real collection, "All rings" in the dropdown, Home, Contact and Journal moved out. Point the hero CTA to Montana sapphire.
5. **C4-06 / C4-07 / C4-08:** Jewelry purchase confidence: an in-page size guide drawer, a delivery estimate and Shop Pay Installments near the CTA, and gift messaging turned on in the cart.
