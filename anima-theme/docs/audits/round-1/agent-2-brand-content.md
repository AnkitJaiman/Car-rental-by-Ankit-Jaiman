# Agent 2: Brand Identity and Content Audit (Round 1)

Theme: Anima Editorial (`/home/user/Car-rental-by-Ankit-Jaiman/anima-theme`). Store: Anima Jewels, www.animajewel.store, USD, ships to the US only.
Auditor stance: independent and read-only. No theme files were modified.

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL FAIL. Do not publish until the Critical and High items are resolved.**

What passes:
- **Sarah O. content:** none. There is no "Sarah", "SOJ", "Dec16" or Sarah O. copy anywhere in the theme. Every image reference is a `shopify://shop_images/...` file that also appears in the live Anima theme (`anima-logo-solid.png`, `aster-round-montana-sapphire-band-12-25-01.jpg`, `Rings.jpg`, `Necklace-36_1.jpg`, `WhiteDiamonds-EARRING.jpg`, `TEN-BRACELET.jpg`, `Our_Story_Page.jpg`).
- **DEMO leakage:** none. "DEMO" content exists only in the harness (`scratchpad/tools/harness.js:329-336`). The `$105.99` compare-at "Sale" price shown on the local render also comes from the harness (`harness.js:60`). `products.json` has `compareAtPriceRange: null` for every active product, and the theme files contain no such price.
- **Reviews:** none are fabricated. `templates/index.json` → `sections.reviews.blocks` is `{}`. The testimonials, Inspiration Station and social sections all stay hidden from shoppers until real content is added (checked in their Liquid).
- **Removed false claims:** the theme no longer says "Handcrafted in the USA", "BIS Hallmarked" or "never lab-grown".

What fails: a few claims go beyond the verified data, and the theme contradicts the store's legal policies in places. These are mainly the "natural gemstones" generalization, "hypoallergenic", the 48-hour damage window with a replacement promise, the support email, and the free-exchange wording. The ring size chart leaves out size 5, which is sold. Every product page links to the Shopify shipping policy, which is still placeholder text.

## Owner decisions required

1. **Official support email:** `animajewels@animajewel.in` (theme) or `bohosoulart@animajewel.in` (Shopify Contact and Refund policies). Make them the same everywhere (B2-05).
2. **Replace the Shopify shipping policy placeholder** ("hey there this is my shipping policy") before launch. The PDP and footer link to it (B2-01).
3. **Exchanges:** are they free? If so, add that to the Shopify refund policy; if not, soften the theme copy (B2-07).
4. **Damaged items:** confirm whether there is a reporting deadline and whether replacement is guaranteed (B2-04).
5. **"Hypoallergenic":** approve or drop the claim; the default is to drop it (B2-03).
6. **Stated response time:** approve "within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday" or remove it (B2-08). Also confirm whether care instructions ship with orders (B2-09).
7. **Origin statement:** still open from research doc §2.1. The theme currently makes no origin claim, which is the safe default. Do not reintroduce "Handcrafted in the USA" unless it is true, given the Jaipur, India business address and the `india made ring` tag.
8. **"BIS Hallmarked":** not in the store data. Keep it out unless the owner provides certification (research doc §2.2).
9. **Hand-finished or hand-set claim** for the moissanite line (B2-15), and **gemstone treatment** disclosure (B2-16).
10. **Moissanite inventory** at 0: restock or untrack before featuring the collection on the homepage (B2-13).
11. **Free-shipping threshold:** confirm $35 is configured in Shopify shipping rates (B2-14).
12. **Image provenance:** confirm `Necklace-36_1.jpg` and `WhiteDiamonds-EARRING.jpg` are Anima's own photos. Remove `SOJ_APR24-0769.jpg` and `Dec16_lo-093.jpg` from the store's collection images (research doc §2.4; the new theme does not reference them).
13. **Social URLs** (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest) and real customer or creator media, with permission, for Inspiration Station and Social. Reviews must stay real-only (research doc §2.8–2.9).
