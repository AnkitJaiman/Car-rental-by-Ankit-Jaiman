# Agent 2: Brand trust and customer credibility (live audit, 2026-11-14)

Site: https://animajewel.store. Evidence: raw HTML/JSON in `site-audit/2/raw/`, screenshots in `site-audit/2/` (m-product.png, m-checkout.png, m-story.png, d-home.png, d-footer.png), checkout text in `site-audit/2/checkout.txt`.
How I sampled: all 157 products from /products.json (12,648 variants), with 16 products read in full. I also checked /meta.json, every /pages/* and /policies/* URL, and the first checkout page (a test item was added to the cart; no details were entered).

## Score: 24 / 100
The theme looks premium, and the brand pages agree with each other (30-day free returns, US only, 925 silver, Montana sapphire as the lead story). Behind that, a US shopper about to spend $150–$500 would hit these problems:
1. Checkout cannot take payment.
2. Four of the five Shopify legal policies return 404.
3. Products called "Diamond" admit in their own text that the stones may be CZ.
4. Every variant shows "Only 3 left".
5. Product copy was pasted from Etsy listings. It contradicts the site's shipping promises and shows a business in India that the brand pages never mention.
6. There are no reviews anywhere.
The brand pages are well built, but the store does not yet back them up.

## Verified facts
- /meta.json: store city Jaipur, Rajasthan, IN; currency USD; `ships_to_countries:["US"]`; `shopify_pay_enabled_card_brands: []`; `offers_shop_pay_installments: false`; store description empty.
- Checkout (m-checkout.png): "This store can't accept payments right now." The checkout footer links only to the Privacy policy.
- Policy URLs: /policies/privacy-policy = 200. /policies/refund-policy, /policies/shipping-policy, /policies/terms-of-service and /policies/contact-information = **404**. The theme's own pages (/pages/refund-policy, /pages/shipping-policy, /pages/cancel-order, /pages/faq) do exist.
- Inventory: all 12,648 variants show available=true. The "sold out" issue in the brief is **fixed**. But every variant renders "Only 3 left" (counted 160/160 on one ring page; same on 3 others).
- All 157 products were created and published on 2026-11-13. All 890 images have Etsy export filenames (`il_fullxfull.*`) and none has alt text.
- Price range $129.13–$785.99, median $207.16. 84% of products are priced $150–$300. Only 7 of 157 prices end in .00/.95/.99. No compare-at prices.
- The title tag is now "Anima Jewels", not "My Store". The site's support email is now support@animajewel.store, **except** in the Privacy Policy, which still says sale@animajewel.store.

## What is working (kept short)
- The brand pages agree with each other: 30-day free returns, a 24-hour cancellation window, US only, and the $35 free-shipping threshold appear the same way on the home page, PDP accordion, FAQ, Contact and Refund pages.
- 925 sterling silver and "plated over silver, not brass" are stated clearly, and the FAQ separates natural sapphire/emerald from lab-created moissanite.
- All products have descriptions (median about 2,200 characters) and about 6 images each. Nothing is sold out any more.
- A privacy-choices (opt-out) page exists, and the privacy policy is current (dated 2026-11-14).

## Could NOT verify
- Whether the stones really are natural, untreated or diamond. The supplier's real dispatch location and carrier. Actual stock levels.
- Shopify admin settings (Payments onboarding status, Taxes, Markets, Policies editor). These were inferred from public endpoints and checkout.
- Instagram profile content (blocked by a login redirect). Off-site reputation (Trustpilot, Google reviews, Etsy shop reviews) was not searched.
- The checkout after the address step (shipping rates, taxes, duties), because no address was entered and payments are disabled.
