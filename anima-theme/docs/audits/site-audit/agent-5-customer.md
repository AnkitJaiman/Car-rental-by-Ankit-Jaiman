# Agent 5: Real US customer perspective (live site, 2026-11-14)

Method: Playwright (Chromium via proxy) at 390x844 (isMobile, hasTouch, iPhone UA) and 1440x900. Journey run on both:
Home → /collections/montana-sapphire-jewelry → /products/big-natural-oval-montana-sapphire-ring ($259.24) → Size guide → Metal "Gold-Plated 925 Silver" + size 7 → Add to bag → cart drawer → /cart → Checkout (first page, observed only; nothing typed, no order placed). Also tested quick-add from the collection grid, and the policy, contact, FAQ, story and size-guide pages, plus /products.json.
Screenshots and scripts are in site-audit/5/ (d-* = desktop, m-* = mobile). Key files: `d-09-payment-crop.png`, `d-03-pdp-fold.png`, `m-03-pdp-fold.png`, `m-03-pdp-mid.png`, `m-10-quickadd.png`, `d-07-cart-drawer.png`, `d-05-sizeguide.png`, `m-09-checkout.png`.

Re-check of known context: every variant is now available (12,648 of 12,648 variants on /products.json show available=true, so nothing is "Sold out"). The footer, contact and cancel pages show support@animajewel.store, and no sale@ address appears. The homepage title is "Anima Jewels" (not "My Store"), and checkout shows "Anima Jewels" as plain text with no logo.

## Score: 38 / 100 (would a real US shopper buy today?)
The storefront looks better than most small Shopify stores. The editorial theme is calm, the photos are attractive, the trust strip is clear (free shipping over $35, free 30-day returns, 925 silver, nickel-free), the size guide opens in a drawer, and the cart drawer is clean and includes a gift note field. **But no one can buy:** checkout shows "This store can't accept payments right now" and the Pay now button is disabled. That alone caps the score. Even with payments fixed, a $150–$500 purchase from an unknown brand is held back by these gaps: zero reviews, no stone size or carat on most listings, a default ring size of 2, "Only 3 left" on every single variant, and no visible people, place or company behind the store.

## 5-second impression
- Desktop/mobile hero: "Montana sapphire, made for every day". It is clear, premium-feeling and on-niche. Positive.
- The logo reads "Anima / Jewelry and Accessories" with a clip-art diamond. The small text can't be read at header size, and it doesn't match the "Anima Jewels" name. It looks less premium than the rest of the page.
- The prices ($216.67, $259.24, $323.53) look auto-calculated rather than set on purpose. That clashes with "honest pricing".

## What worked (kept short)
- The add-to-bag → drawer flow is fast and correct (variant "Gold-Plated 925 Silver / 7", $259.24, "You have unlocked free shipping", gift note field, checkout + view bag).
- The /cart page and checkout totals matched ($259.24, no surprise fees shown before the address step).
- The free US shipping over $35 and free 30-day returns message is consistent across the bar, PDP, policies and FAQ.
- The size guide opens in a drawer without leaving the PDP.

## Could NOT verify
- Shipping rates or taxes at checkout (no address entered, by design), and which payment methods will appear once payments are active.
- Whether the "Only 3 left" stock numbers are real (inventory counts are not public), and whether image rights belong to the owner (the Etsy file names suggest the owner's Etsy shop, but this is unconfirmed).
- Instagram/Google landing behaviour from the real apps (not fetched), so I simulated it by direct URL.
- Performance was not formally measured. Only Playwright load-event times were observed: home 2.0 s, collection 1.8 s, PDP 0.7 s on desktop over the proxy.
- Order/confirmation emails, post-purchase experience, and customer service response time.
