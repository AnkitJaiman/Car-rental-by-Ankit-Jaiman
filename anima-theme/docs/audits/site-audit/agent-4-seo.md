# Agent 4: Google SEO, Technical SEO and E-commerce Growth audit (live site, 2026-11-14)

Scope: https://animajewel.store fetched live (curl + Playwright Chromium 390x844 mobile and 1440x900 desktop). Evidence: `site-audit/4/` (raw JSON, HTML snapshots, `shots/*.png`, `shots/perf.json`, scripts).

## Score: 48 / 100 (SEO + commerce readiness)
The technical base is good: clean canonicals, unique titles in the right length range, Shopify sitemaps, BreadcrumbList/Organization/WebSite/FAQ JSON-LD, WebP/AVIF images, CLS 0. Two problems stop the store from selling. **Checkout cannot take payment.** A **hidden 70% automatic discount** means the price on every product page, in the structured data and in any product feed is 3.3x what the customer actually pays. Product copy also still carries Etsy-era claims ("Worldwide shipping", "Only one available", "Diamond (or CZ)") that contradict the site's own policies. That breaks Merchant Center rules and the FTC Jewelry Guides. Analytics and ad pixels are missing, the blog is empty and UK/Canada are not set up.

**Counts by priority:** Critical 2 · High 7 · Medium 9 · Low 4 (22 issues)

## Verified facts (measured)
- robots.txt is Shopify's default (2026 agent-aware version). It blocks cart, checkout, account and the sort/filter traps. It does **not** block `/search`.
- sitemap.xml is an index of 5 child sitemaps. Products: 158 locs (157 products plus `/`), 157 image entries. Collections: 14 (includes `/collections/frontpage`). Pages: 9. Blogs: 1 (`/blogs/news` only, **0 articles**). There is also an agentic discovery sitemap (`/agents.md`).
- 157 products, all with vendor "Anima Jewels". **12,648 variants, all `available=true`** (the earlier "all sold out" finding is now fixed). Variants per product: median 104, max 162. Rings carry 53 sizes (2 to 15) x 3 metals. **0 of 12,648 variants have a SKU.** 0 have a compare-at price.
- Product descriptions run 129 to 1,036 words (median 338). Nothing is thin by word count, but the copy is templated (see A4-05).
- Titles: home "Anima Jewels" (12 chars). Collection titles are 47 to 57 chars and descriptions 144 to 157 chars, both well written. PDP titles are 43 to 55 chars. **No "My Store" leakage**: og:site_name is "Anima Jewels" everywhere. **No `sale@` email** anywhere. Pages show support@animajewel.store.
- Canonicals: self-referencing on every sampled page. Collection-scoped product URLs (`/collections/rings/products/x`) return 200 with canonical `/products/x`. `/collections/all/products/x` returns 301 to `/products/x`. www returns 301 to the apex. I could not test http to https because the proxy refused plain HTTP.
- Link check: 162 unique internal non-product URLs plus product links. 0 broken links. 1 `/account` 302 to Shopify customer accounts (expected), several 429 rate limits during the crawl, and 301s from `/collections/all/products/*`. `/policies/refund-policy`, `/policies/shipping-policy`, `/policies/terms-of-service` and `/policies/contact-information` all return **404**. Only `/policies/privacy-policy` returns 200.
- H1: exactly one per page and on topic. Home H1 is "Montana sapphire, made for every day". Collection pages repeat the H1 text as an H2.
- JSON-LD parsed on every sampled page with no parse errors. Home: Organization (logo, Instagram sameAs) and WebSite with SearchAction. Collections: BreadcrumbList only. PDP: Product (name, url, 5 images, description, brand) with one Offer per variant (159 on a ring), each with InStock, NewCondition, price and USD, plus BreadcrumbList. FAQ page: FAQPage. **Product schema is missing**: sku, gtin/mpn, shippingDetails, hasMerchantReturnPolicy, aggregateRating/review (no reviews exist) and priceValidUntil.
- Pixels: the `webPixelsConfigList` holds only the Shopify app pixel and one **custom pixel**. **No GA4/gtag, Google Ads, GTM, Meta, TikTok or Pinterest** in the page or the pixel config. No google-site-verification meta tag (Search Console may be verified by DNS; not checked). Shop countryCode is **IN**.
- Cart test (moissanite studs, $131.97). The drawer opened with "Your bag (1)", a free-shipping progress message, a gift note field and **"70% off storewide -$92.37", subtotal $39.60**. `/cart` shows the same. There is no discount code field and no shipping estimator in the cart. The accelerated buttons (Buy it now) appear on the PDP only.
- Checkout, first page only (screenshot `shots/d-checkout.png`): branded "Anima Jewels". Country list is **United States only**. "Email me with news and offers" is **pre-ticked**. **There is no discount code field.** Payment block reads **"This store can't accept payments right now."** "Pay now" is disabled. The footer links only the Privacy policy.
- PDP: price, "Free US shipping over $35", "Free 30-day returns", and a sticky add-to-bag bar on mobile. **No reviews.** Shows **"Tax included."** Related products load asynchronously; the endpoint returned 4 items. Recently viewed is present.
- Collections: 48 cards per page, paginated, sort control, 4 filter inputs, plus a 12 to 22 word intro.
- Search: `/search?q=tennis bracelet` returns 7 results. Predictive suggestions exist (Montana sapphire, Emerald, Moissanite ring, Tennis bracelet).
- Email capture: footer "Join our list" form. No popup appeared within 8 s.
