# Agent 1: UI/UX and visual design audit, animajewel.store
Date: 2026-11-14. Method: Playwright (Chromium) on the live site at 390x844 (mobile, touch), 768x1024 (tablet) and 1440x900 (desktop). I took screenshots and looked at them, ran hover, menu, filter and add-to-bag checks, captured console and network errors, and pulled /products.json (157 products, 890 images, 12,648 variants). Screenshots and scripts are in `site-audit/1/`. I did not edit any theme files.

## Score: 62 / 100
The theme itself is close to premium. It has a restrained palette (black, white, warm ivory), a sensible type pairing (Playfair Display for headings, Assistant for body text), generous spacing, quick-add buttons that appear on hover, image swap on hover, a calm marquee that respects reduced-motion settings, a clean cart drawer, and a good 404 page. I found no broken images, no horizontal scroll at 390, 768 or 1440, and no theme JavaScript errors. The things that make it look cheaper than Mejuri, Catbird, Monica Vinader or Aurate are mostly about brand and merchandising, not layout:
- A 70% discount that only shows up in the cart.
- A clip-art logo.
- Prices with odd cents.
- Etsy-exported lifestyle photos with no consistent product-shot style.
- Ring size preselected to "2".
- Variant photos that never change when you pick a metal.

Fix these and the site could score in the low 80s without a redesign.

Counts by priority: **Critical 1 · High 6 · Medium 9 · Low 8** (24 issues)

## What I verified (facts)
- All 12,648 variants are now `available: true`. The earlier "Sold out" state no longer applies; the Rings filter shows "In stock 78 / Out of stock 0".
- Store name is fixed. Titles read "… | Anima Jewels" and the footer reads "© 2026 Anima Jewels".
- Support email shows **support@animajewel.store** in the footer, the mobile menu and 4 other places. sale@ does not appear in the homepage HTML.
- Every nav and footer link returned 200. /pages/about is 404, but nothing links to it ("Our Story" points to /pages/our-story, which returns 200).
- Console: the only error is `Framing 'https://shop.app/' violates CSP frame-ancestors` plus a 403 on shop.app/pay/hop. This is Shopify's Shop Pay sign-in iframe, comes from Shopify's side, and is harmless to shoppers. Analytics beacons were aborted by the headless browser. There are no theme JS errors.
- Images: 0 broken. 14 images on the homepage and 7 on the PDP have empty alt text.

## What works (keep it)
- An editorial layout with real white space, and a consistent eyebrow + serif heading system across sections.
- Hover image swap and hover-only "Choose options" on desktop; a "+" quick-add on touch.
- The marquee pauses on hover and stops under prefers-reduced-motion (base.css:889-890). The announcement fade is subtle.
- Cart drawer: a free-shipping progress message, a gift-note option and "Secure checkout by Shopify".
- The mobile menu and footer accordions are clean, and tap targets are mostly 44px.

## Could not verify
- Lighthouse, LCP and CLS: not measured by me (Agent 1). See the performance agent.
- Checkout pages beyond the cart drawer, account and login pages, and the order-status page.
- Whether the Etsy-exported images belong to the owner (I can't tell; flagged only as a consistency issue).
- Safari/iOS rendering (Chromium only), real-device touch feel, and whether autoplay sliders behave well on low-power devices.
- Whether the 70% automatic discount is intentional, or how long it has been running (no admin access).
